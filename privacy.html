Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

Safar is a trip planner. It is built so that as much as possible happens on your own device. This page says exactly what leaves your device, why, and how to remove it.

In short

  • Your emails and calendar are read on your device. Safar's server never receives them.
  • Safar's server stores only your sign-in identity and your trip, so the trip can follow you between devices.
  • Safar does not sell data, show ads, or use your data to train AI models.
  • You can delete your account and trip at any time from Settings.

Google and Microsoft account data

When you sign in, Safar asks for permission to read your email and to read and add events in your calendar.

  • Email. Safar searches for travel confirmations (flights, trains, hotel and Airbnb bookings) and reads those messages in your browser to pull out dates, places and booking details. It does not send, delete or change email.
  • Calendar. Safar reads travel-related events and, when you ask it to, adds your plan to your calendar.
  • Email and calendar content is processed on your device and is not sent to Safar's server.

Safar's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Safar does not use Google user data for advertising, does not sell it, and does not use it to develop or train generalised AI or machine-learning models. No person at Safar reads your data unless you ask for support and give permission, or it is required for security or by law.

If you connect an AI assistant

Connecting Claude or ChatGPT is optional and uses your own key. When it is connected, the questions you ask, a summary of your trip, and the text of booking emails that Safar could not read by itself are sent from your device directly to the provider you chose (Anthropic or OpenAI) and handled under that provider's terms. If you do not connect an AI assistant, none of this is sent.

Services Safar calls

To draw maps and find places, your device sends requests such as place names, map areas and coordinates to these services. They receive your IP address, as any website does, but not your name or account.

  • OpenFreeMap and OpenStreetMap (map tiles), Photon and Nominatim (place search), Overpass (cafés and restaurants), OSRM (routes)
  • Wikipedia (sights and photos), Open-Meteo (weather), adsbdb (flight routes)
  • Booking and comparison sites — such as Airbnb, KAYAK, Expedia and Turo — only when you tap a button that opens them; the link carries the place and dates of that search, never your name or account
  • Through Safar's server, so they never see your IP address or account: Transit (public transport departures near you while you navigate by metro or bus; your position is rounded to about 100 metres) and LiteAPI (hotel prices for a city and your dates)
  • Google and Microsoft sign-in services, and Uber, Apple Maps or Google Maps only when you tap a button that opens them

Safar's server

The server runs on Cloudflare. It stores your account record and one copy of your trip, plus one previous copy as a safety backup. It also keeps a short-lived cache of public map lookups, crash reports (described above, kept 30 days) and daily request counts used to stay within the hosting plan; none of these contain your trip or account. Trips are sent over an encrypted connection. A weekly encrypted backup of accounts and trips is kept for 90 days, so data can be restored after a fault.

If you buy Safar Pro

Payments are handled by Stripe, which receives your card details, email and billing address directly; Safar never sees or stores your card. Safar's server keeps only what it needs to switch Pro on: your Safar account, the plan, when it ends and Stripe's customer reference. Purchase records are kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires, even if you delete your account. Stripe's own privacy policy is at stripe.com/privacy.

Sharing a trip

A share link contains the trip itself. Anyone who has the link can open that copy. Share links never contain your sign-in tokens, account details or keys.

Keeping and deleting

  • Delete everything: Settings → Delete account removes your account record and trip from the server and clears the trip from the device.
  • Sign out: removes the sign-in tokens from the device.
  • Withdraw access: you can also remove Safar's access in your Google or Microsoft account settings at any time.
  • Server data is kept until you delete it. You can also ask for deletion or a copy by email.

Children

Safar is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
