How it works

  1. Where

    Add your cities and nights, and your flight or train. Signed in, Safar fills these in from your confirmation emails.

  2. Explore and stay

    Pick the sights you want from each city's best-known places, and choose a hotel or your Airbnb.

  3. Plan

    Safar orders each day so you don't double back, checks opening hours, and fits in lunch, dinner and coffee breaks.

  4. Go

    On the day, Safar guides you to the next stop and adjusts the rest of the day if you run late.

Made for the way you travel

Your bookings, found for you

Flights, trains, hotels and Airbnb stays come in from Gmail or Outlook. Emails are read on your phone, never stored on our server.

Your own pace

Set when your day starts and ends, how long lunch is and how often you want a break.

Travel together

Share a live trip: everyone with the link sees the same plan, and edits reach the others within about a minute.

Works offline

Save the map for your cities before you leave, and the plan and directions keep working with no signal.

In your calendar

One tap puts each day's stops into Google Calendar or Outlook, in the city's own time zone.

Five languages

English, French, Spanish, Farsi and Arabic.

Plan in Claude or ChatGPT

Add Safar as a connector and ask for a trip in plain words. You get a day-by-day outline and a link that opens it in Safar.
