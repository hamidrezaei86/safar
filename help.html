Quick answers about planning, bookings, sharing and your data. Still stuck? Write to us at the bottom of this page.

Planning

How does Safar decide the order of my day?

It looks at the places you picked, the time it takes to get between them, opening hours, your lunch and dinner times and your breaks, then finds the order with the least travelling. Must-see places come first if a day is too full.

Do I have to choose a hotel?

Yes, one per city. Each day starts and ends there. If you're in an Airbnb, add it under Stay with "Booked an Airbnb?" — you can type the address from your booking.

A place is shown as closed that day. What can I do?

Opening hours come from OpenStreetMap. Open the place from your list in Explore, check the hours, and set "Only on" to a day it's open. If the hours are wrong, change Opens and Closes there.

Can I change my pace, meals and breaks?

Yes. In Settings, drag the handles in "Day rhythm" to set when your day starts and ends and when you eat, and choose how often you want a break.

Can I plan with Claude or ChatGPT?

Yes. Add Safar as a connector using https://safar-api.hamid-ebb.workers.dev/mcp, then ask for a trip. You'll get a day-by-day outline and a link that opens the trip in Safar.

Bookings and accounts

How does Safar find my flights and hotels?

When you sign in with Google or Microsoft, Safar searches your email and calendar for travel confirmations and reads them on your phone. It never stores your emails on its server, and never sends, changes or deletes email.

Do I need an account?

No. Without one, everything stays on your phone. Signing in finds your bookings, adds your plan to your calendar, and keeps your trip on all your devices.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Delete account removes your account and trip from Safar's server. You can also remove Safar's access in your Google or Microsoft account settings.

On the trip

Will it work without mobile data?

Yes, if you save the map first: in Plan, tap "Save map offline" on Wi-Fi before you leave. The plan, map and navigation then work with no signal.

How do I add the plan to my calendar?

In Plan, tap "Add to calendar". If you're signed in, the stops go straight into your Google Calendar or Outlook, in the city's time zone. Tapping it again replaces the earlier version. Without an account, you get a calendar file.

Can I travel with friends on one plan?

Yes. In Plan, tap Share trip → Start live trip, then send the edit link. Everyone sees the same trip, and changes reach the others within about a minute. Only you can stop sharing.

How do I rent a car?

In Where, tap "Need a rental car?". Safar fills in your pick-up place and dates and opens KAYAK, Expedia or Turo to compare prices. Once booked, add it so pick-up and return appear in your plan.

How do I change the language?

Settings → Language. Safar speaks English, French, Spanish, Farsi and Arabic.
